946: <span>How does Mini Gastric Bypass differ from standard Roux-en-Y Gastric Bypass?</span>
952: <p>While standard Roux-en-Y Gastric Bypass requires two surgical connections (anastomoses) and divides the small bowel into a Y-shape, Mini Gastric Bypass requires only ONE connection between the stomach pouch and an intact loop of intestine. This single-anastomosis design reduces surgical operating time by 30-40 minutes, minimizes anesthesia exposure, and significantly lowers the long-term risk of internal hernias.</p>
963: <p>In his peer-reviewed research published in <em>Obesity Surgery</em> (2014) titled <em>"MGB vs RYGB: 1 Year Outcomes in Super Obese"</em>, Dr. Peters demonstrated that Mini Gastric Bypass achieved superior weight loss velocity and equally high comorbidity remission rates compared to Roux-en-Y in super obese cohorts (BMI ≥ 50), with significantly shorter operative time and hospital length of stay.</p>
