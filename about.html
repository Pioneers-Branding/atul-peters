About the Surgeon

Transforming Lives Through Advanced Surgery

Dr. Atul N. C. Peters joined Max Smart Super Speciality Hospital, Saket – New Delhi in June 2019 as Director – Dept. of Bariatric, Minimal Access & General Surgery and the same under his patronship got awarded as CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE for Bariatric and Metabolic Surgery by OSSI effective from 01.01.2020 and also got awarded as CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE for Robotic and Innovative Surgery by ARIS effective from 02.03.2024. Dr. Peters is also a ‘Proctor’ in Robotic surgery and provides real-time guidance, feedback and support to a trainee surgeon during a robotic procedure, focusing on technical skills and practical aspects of using the robotic system.

He is a DHA Certified, Specialist Bariatric and Robotic Surgeon at New Modern Hospital, Dubai. He is a consultant Bariatric Surgeon at NMC, Muscat. With close to three decades of surgical experience, his practice is now dedicated to advance GI, minimal access and robotic surgery with special interest in Bariatric & Metabolic Surgery for diabetes, performing over 1500 cases annually.

Dr. Peters has been accredited the ‘’Surgeon of Excellence’’ by Surgical Review Corporation, USA that administers Surgeon of Excellence programs throughout the world and recognizes the surgeons and healthcare facilities that provide safe, high quality surgical care. These programs promote universal care quality, regardless of where the patients have their procedures performed. He is also the founder member of ACMOMS (Asian Consensus Meeting on Metabolic Surgery).

Beyond the OR

A Life of Discipline & Art

Operating is one chapter. The other chapters — teaching, music, fitness — shape the surgeon.

01

The Teacher

For Dr. Atul Peters, surgery is not just about mastering a skill, it is about sharing knowledge and empowering the next generation. Over the years, teaching and mentorship have become an integral part of his journey as a surgeon. He believes that the best teachers do more than impart knowledge; they encourage curiosity, challenge conventional thinking and inspire others to strive for excellence.

From mentoring young surgeons to sharing his experience in bariatric, metabolic, minimal access and robotic surgery, Dr. Peters remains deeply committed to nurturing the next generation of medical professionals. For him, every teaching opportunity is an opportunity to give back to the profession that has given him so much.

Education
02

The Musician

Beyond the precision of the operating room lies a deeply creative side of Dr. Peters. Music has always been an important part of his life, a space where he finds expression, joy and balance.

A multi-instrumentalist, he enjoys playing the saxophone, flute, harmonica, piano, drums and guitar. Whether playing for himself, his family, friends or his patients, music brings out a different side of his personality, one that is spontaneous, expressive and deeply connected to the joy of living.

For Dr. Peters, music is more than a hobby. It is a form of therapy, a way to connect with people and a reminder that life, like music, is best experienced with rhythm, passion and presence.

Art
03

The Fitness Freak

Fitness is not a phase for Dr. Peters, it is a way of life. With more than 30 years of weight training behind him, fitness has been a constant companion through the many demands of his surgical career.

A certified fitness trainer and a practitioner of Krav Maga, he believes that physical strength is built through consistency, discipline and commitment. His approach to fitness goes beyond looking strong, it is about staying energetic, resilient and mentally sharp.

For Dr. Peters, the discipline that drives him in the gym is the same discipline he brings to the operating room: preparation, focus, precision and the determination to keep pushing his limits. His fitness journey is a reflection of his belief that taking care of oneself is not a luxury, it is an essential part of living a healthier, more purposeful life.
