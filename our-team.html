Meet Our Team

Dedicated experts in Bariatric, Minimal Access, and Robotic Surgery providing world-class care and support.

Dr. Yogesh Gautam

Associate Director & Advisor

Dr. Yogesh Gautam is Associate Director & Advisor at the Institute of Bariatric & Metabolic Surgery. He graduated from Delhi University in the year 1978 and did his Masters in surgery, and Diplomate of National Board Surgery in 1983 and ever since has trained more than 100 surgical students under his guidance. He is one of the pioneers to bring Minimal Access Surgery to India. He has more than 25 years of experience in Minimal Access Surgery and has participated in many teaching programs. He has been associated with tertiary care hospitals such as Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, Batra Hospital and Max Hospital. In Kolmet Hospital, he is instrumental in setting up of the Department of General and Laparoscopic Surgery and leading the academic programmes. He is also an MBA in Healthcare from FMS, Delhi.

Memberships & Fellowships:

  • International Federation for the Surgery of Obesity & Metabolic Disorders (IFSO)
  • Obesity Surgery Society of India (OSSI)
  • Society of American Gastrointestinal & Endoscopic Surgeons (SAGES)
  • Fellow - Indian Association of Gastrointestinal Endo surgeons (FIAGES)
  • Indian Association of Surgical Gastroenterologists
  • Asia Pacific Hernia Society (APHS)
  • Association of Surgeons of India (ASI)

Dr. Atul Wadhwa

Principal Consultant

Dr. Atul Wadhwa Completed His Masters in Surgery From the University of Rajasthan in 1999, following Which He Joined Safdarjung Hospital as a Registrar. After Completing the Requisite Training as a Registrar, He Joined the Fellowship Programme in Minimal Access Surgery Under the Able Guidance of Padmashree Dr. P.K. Chowbey at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital. During His Tenure as a Fellow, He Gained Wide Experience in Basic and Advanced Laparoscopic Surgery. He Has Also Trained Under the Esteemed Prof. Alferd Cuscheri Dundee (UK) in the Field of Upper Gi Laparoscopic Surgery. He Has Authored Various Publications in Reputed International and National Journals and Has Received Best Paper Award. He Is Also Author of Various Chapters in Books on Minimal Access Surgery.he Had to His Credit of Being a Speaker at Various National Conferences. He Is an Active Member of National and International Associations of Laparoscopic and Endoscopic Surgery.

He Has Interest in Abdominal Surgery Including the Whole Spectrum From Minimally Invasive Hepato-biliary, Colo-rectal and Bariatric Surgery. He has been trained in the da Vinci robotic system and is an active member of robotic surgery team. He is presently working as principal consultant in the Dept. of Bariatric, Minimal Access & Robotic Surgery.

Dr. Shalabh Agarwal

Senior Consultant

Dr. Shalabh Agarwal is Senior Consultant in the dept. of Bariatric, Minimal Access & Robotic Surgery. He was a surgical resident at Hindu Rao Hospital, Delhi, where he gained wide experience in general and laparoscopic Surgery. He has attended several specialized training courses in Minimal Access Surgeries in India, followed by Fellowship in Bariatric & Metabolic Surgery at our Institute. He has assisted more than 1000 bariatric surgeries. He was recently awarded the Fellowship by the International Bariatric Club, after successful completion of the advanced Bariatric Surgery course.

Dr. Shalabh is also a trained ACLS/BLS provider, certified by American Heart Association. He has special interest in data management and monitoring of clinical outcomes and surgical audits. He also holds Fellowship in Hernia Surgery (FALS) and a Masters course in Minimal Invasive Proctology by IMAST, Mumbai. He has been trained in the Da Vinci Robotic System and is an active member of the robotic surgery team.

Dr. Saurabh Goswami

Senior Consultant

Dr Saurabh Goswami is presently working as Senior Consultant at the Dept of Bariatric, Minimal Access & Robotic surgery. She began her academic journey completing MBBS from DYPMC, Pune followed by Masters in Gen Surgery from BVP, Pune. Dr. Saurabh holds prestigious fellowships - F.MAS, FALS, FIAGES further honing her expertise. She is also a trained ACLS/BLS provider, certified by American Heart Institute.

Throughout her career, she held pivotal roles in esteemed medical institutes such as PGIMSR ESI Hospital, Basaidarapur and Max Smart Super Speciality, Saket Dr. Saurabh has special interests in laparoscopic and advanced abdominal surgery, breast and colorectal surgeries.

She has been trained in the Da Vinci Robotic System and is an active member of the robotic surgery team. In addition to her skill set, she provides warmth and care to all the ladies with a smile. She also leads specialized clinics addressing medical problems requiring surgical solutions, in ladies.

Dr. Beena Arora, Ph. D.

Chief Dietician

Dr. Beena Arora is the Chief Dietician in the Department of Bariatric, Minimal Access, and Robotic Surgery. She holds a Ph.D. in Food and Nutrition from the University of Delhi, focusing on the nutritional profile and quality of life in bariatric surgery patients. With over two decades of experience, she has worked with reputed hospitals and academic institutions.

Dr. Arora is responsible for pre-operative nutritional assessments and post-operative nutritional care of the patients. She leads the bariatric nutritional program and also is the key person responsible for organizing bariatric support groups. She also runs our non-surgical weight loss program "Think Fit", and specializes in the nutritional care of patients undergoing intra-gastric ballooning. Apart from her clinical duties, she is also involved in bariatric data management and research that underscores her commitment to advancing patient care. She also contributes in public health education through social media content writing and blogging.

She is a faculty member for the CICO (Certification In Integrated Care of Obesity), jointly run by OSSI (Obesity surgery society of India) and IFSO (International Federation for the Surgery of Obesity and Metabolic disorders) and holds life memberships in the IDA (Indian Dietetic Association), OSSI (Obesity and Metabolic Surgery Society of India), ADE (The Association of Diabetes Educators), and IAPEN (Indian Association for Parenteral and Enteral Nutrition).

Ms. Nalini Massey
