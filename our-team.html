Meet Our Team

Dedicated experts in Bariatric, Minimal Access, and Robotic Surgery providing world-class care and support.

Dr. Yogesh Gautam

Associate Director & Advisor

Graduated from Delhi University (1978) with a Masters in Surgery and DNB (1983). A pioneer bringing Minimal Access Surgery to India with over 25 years of experience.

Trained over 100 surgical students and has been instrumental in setting up General and Laparoscopic Surgery departments in top tertiary care hospitals.

Dr. Atul Wadhwa

Principal Consultant

Completed Masters in Surgery (1999) and Fellowship in Minimal Access Surgery under Padmashree Dr. P.K. Chowbey. Trained under Prof. Alfred Cuschieri (UK) in Upper GI Laparoscopic Surgery.

Active member of the robotic surgery team, trained in the da Vinci robotic system. Specializes in Minimally Invasive Hepato-biliary, Colo-rectal, and Bariatric Surgery.

Dr. Shalabh Agarwal

Senior Consultant

Fellow of the International Bariatric Club. Assisted over 1000 bariatric surgeries and holds a Fellowship in Hernia Surgery (FALS).

Certified ACLS/BLS provider by the American Heart Association. Special interest in data management, clinical outcomes, and surgical audits. Trained in the Da Vinci Robotic System.

Dr. Saurabh Goswami

Senior Consultant

Holds prestigious fellowships (F.MAS, FALS, FIAGES). Specializes in laparoscopic and advanced abdominal surgery, breast, and colorectal surgeries.

Active member of the robotic surgery team. Leads specialized clinics addressing medical problems requiring surgical solutions specifically for women.

Dr. Beena Arora, Ph. D.

Chief Dietician

Ph.D. in Food and Nutrition (University of Delhi). Responsible for pre-operative nutritional assessments and post-operative nutritional care.

Leads the bariatric nutritional program and support groups. Faculty member for CICO and runs the non-surgical weight loss program "Think Fit".

Ms. Nalini Massey

Program Manager

Associated with the institute for over 14 years. Primary contact person for patients, organizing workshops, CMEs, and academic activities.

Coordinates with all departments for smooth patient scheduling. Key role in handling international patients for travel, stay, and treatment coordination.
